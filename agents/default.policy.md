# Default Email Agent Policy

Applies when no contact-specific or company-specific policy exists.

## High priority

- Direct questions addressed to you
- Deadline or blocker language
- Messages from contacts marked `importance: high`

## Never auto-send

- Legal, pricing, HIPAA, security, or contractual commitments
- Patient-level or PHI content
- Scope changes without your review

## Draft tone

- Concise, professional, warm, action-oriented
