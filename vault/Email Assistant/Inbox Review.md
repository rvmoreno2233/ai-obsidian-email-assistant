## Daily workflow

1. Scan new entries under **Inbox Items**
2. Open linked company/contact/project notes for context
3. Approve or edit drafts in [[Email Assistant/Draft Replies]]
4. Mark waiting items complete in [[Email Assistant/Waiting For]] when resolved
